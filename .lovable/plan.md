

## Most Perfect — Interactive Book Guide App

### Overview
A clean, minimal interactive course experience that turns the book's 6 parts and 29 chapters into a guided journey with progress tracking, chapter navigation, and key content highlights.

### Pages & Features

#### 1. **Home / Welcome Page**
- Book title, subtitle, and a compelling excerpt from the Prologue
- "Begin Your Journey" CTA button
- Visual progress indicator showing overall completion
- Quick-jump to any Part (I–VI)

#### 2. **Course Overview / Table of Contents**
- All 6 Parts displayed as expandable sections
- Each Part shows its chapters with brief descriptions
- Progress checkmarks for completed chapters
- Clean card-based layout with part numbers and titles

#### 3. **Chapter Reader View**
- Clean reading experience with the chapter's full content
- Chapter title, part label, and chapter number header
- Key bullet points and teachings rendered beautifully
- "Mark as Complete" button at the bottom
- Previous/Next chapter navigation
- Sidebar with chapter list for quick navigation (collapsible on mobile)

#### 4. **Progress Dashboard**
- Visual progress bar per Part (I–VI)
- Overall completion percentage
- List of completed vs remaining chapters
- "Continue where you left off" button
- Stats: chapters completed, current streak concept

#### 5. **Key Concepts & Laws Reference**
- Dedicated page for "The 10 Laws of the Proven Life" (Chapter 10)
- Quick-reference cards for core concepts: Most Perfect, Above Human, Proven and Verified
- Searchable/filterable by Part or theme

### Content Structure
- All 29 chapters + Prologue + Epilogue content from the PDF
- Organized into 6 Parts as defined in the book
- Key quotes highlighted throughout

### Design
- Clean white backgrounds with generous whitespace
- Elegant serif font for chapter headings, clean sans-serif for body
- Subtle progress indicators (thin accent bars)
- Minimal color palette: dark text, one accent color for CTAs and progress
- Smooth page transitions
- Fully responsive (mobile-first reading experience)

### Data Persistence
- Progress saved to localStorage (no backend needed)
- Bookmarking current position
